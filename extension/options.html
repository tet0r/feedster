Feedster

Shows your unread count in the toolbar. Click the icon to see your latest unread articles and open Feedster.

Setting up
  1. In Feedster, open Settings → Reading apps and choose a username and app password.
  2. Enter your Feedster address, that username and app password here, then Connect.
  3. Allow access to your Feedster address when the browser asks. The extension can't see any other site.
